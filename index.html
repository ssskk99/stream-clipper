Stream Clipper

A personal tool that turns Twitch stream highlights into vertical short videos and uploads them to its owner's YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Stream Clipper runs on its owner's own Windows PC. When a followed Twitch stream ends, it finds the most interesting moments, cuts them into 1–3 minute vertical clips with captions, and writes a title and description for each one. The owner reviews the clips and, if they choose, uploads them to their own YouTube channel as Shorts and sends them to their own TikTok account as drafts, which they finish and post in the TikTok app. Each upload credits the original streamer and links to the source stream.

Stream Clipper is not a public service. It has no user accounts and no website other than this page, and it is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel and TikTok account it posts to.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Google user data it accesses

Stream Clipper asks for one Google permission: upload videos to your YouTube channel (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). It uses this permission only to upload clips the channel owner has made with the tool, together with each clip's title and description. It does not read, change or delete your existing videos, comments, subscriptions, playlists, or any other account data.

TikTok user data it accesses

Stream Clipper asks TikTok for basic profile access (user.info.basic, which TikTok includes with sign-in) and upload drafts (video.upload). It uses these only to send clips the account owner made with the tool to their own TikTok inbox as drafts. It does not read, post, change or delete anything else on the account.

How it is stored

The sign-in tokens Google and TikTok issue are saved only in files on the owner's own computer. They are never sent anywhere except to Google or TikTok when uploading. Stream Clipper has no servers and collects no analytics.

Sharing

Stream Clipper does not sell, share or transfer Google or TikTok user data to anyone. To pick highlights, it sends text transcripts and chat activity from public Twitch streams to Anthropic's Claude API. This is public stream content, not Google or TikTok user data.

Limited Use

Stream Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke Stream Clipper's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions (TikTok). Deleting the token files from the computer also removes them permanently.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. For questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
